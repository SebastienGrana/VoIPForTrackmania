To test with two tabs without a second TM account: on this second tab, check this box and enter the login of tab 1 (the one in "position from game" mode). Your position is then computed automatically and adjustable in real time.

You are always at the center. Other players appear in red (opacity = computed volume), and the view auto-scales to the max range ring. Without "position from game" mode, drag the blue dot to move yourself. The mic starts muted to avoid feedback when testing two tabs on the same machine.
